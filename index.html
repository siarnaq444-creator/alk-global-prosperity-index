Alk Global Prosperity Index (2021-2025)

Developed by JoAlBrIn • Open-Access Research Project

A multidimensional isotropic framework designed to evaluate and rank the structural performance of 198 sovereign jurisdictions. By balancing 25,000+ data points across four equally-weighted macro-pillars, this platform uncovers the real trade-offs between macroeconomic growth, civic safety, and human development.
